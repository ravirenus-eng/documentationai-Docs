## Problem

- Can't open documentation pages
- See "Access Denied" or blank screen

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Login Status">
    Are you signed in to your account at `ravikumar.com`?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Permissions">
    Ensure you have access rights for the requested project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear Cache">
    Log out, clear browser cache, and log in again.
  </Step>
</Steps>
