description: Arrange and manage documentation stories for your projects.

## Overview

Structure your documentation for better clarity.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a New Story">
    In your dashboard, click "New Story" and give it a descriptive title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange Stories">
    Drag and drop stories to reorder them as you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Categorize">
    Group related stories using folders or tags for easy navigation.
  </Step>
</Steps>
