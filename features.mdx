## Overview

Discover the core features of Ravi Kumar's educational comics platform. You get access to specially curated stories that blend real-world knowledge with engaging narratives. Designed for curious minds, these comics cover science, history, business, psychology, and technology. Build interconnected thinking while fostering emotional intelligence (`EQ`) for an AI-driven world. Customize comics with your child as the lead character and order print-on-demand copies delivered to your home.

<Callout kind="info">
  All content is created by experts: science by IITians, business insights by MBAs.
</Callout>
