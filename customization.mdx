## Step-by-Step Guide

Follow these steps to create your personalized comic.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access the Tool" icon="login">
    Log in to your account at `https://app.ravikumar.com/dashboard`.

    Navigate to the <kbd>Library</kbd> tab and click <kbd>Customize Comic</kbd>.

    <Callout kind="tip">
      New users start with a free trial comic.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Base Comic" icon="search">
    Browse categories and choose a title like "Agastya's Science Quest".

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Web" icon="globe">
        Use filters for age group and topic.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Mobile App" icon="smartphone">
        Tap categories in the bottom navigation.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Input Child Details" icon="user">
    Enter name (e.g., "Alex"), age (6-12), hair color, and photo upload.

    Optional: Add hobbies like "loves dogs" for story integration.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview Version" icon="eye">
    Generate a digital preview (takes `<30s`).

    Review first 5 pages and edit details if needed.

    <Expandable title="Preview Tips" default-open="true">
      Zoom to check character likeness. Share preview link with family.
    </Expandable>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Order and Print" icon="shopping-cart">
    Select quantity (1-10 copies) and delivery address.

    Complete payment. Expect delivery in 5-7 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Preview Not Loading" default-open="true">
    Clear browser cache or try incognito mode. Ensure internet speed `>5Mbps`.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Character Looks Wrong">
    Re-upload a clear, front-facing photo. Avoid filters or hats.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
