## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">

Before you begin, ensure you have:
- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- A valid email address for account creation
- Internet connection for accessing comics

This quickstart guides you through account setup and your first comic experience.

</Callout>

## Create and Sign In to Your Account

Follow these steps to get started with Ravi Kumar's comic platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Home Page" icon="home">
    Open your browser and navigate to the [Ravi Kumar platform](https://preview.omahaindia.com/).
    
    Click the **Sign In / Join** button in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Select **Join** or **Sign Up**.
    
    Enter your email, create a password, and provide basic details like name.
    
    Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In" icon="log-in">
    Return to the sign-in page and enter your credentials.
    
    You'll land on the dashboard, ready to explore.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Navigate Home and Library

Use the top navigation to switch between sections.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Home" icon="home">
    The home page features hero images, featured comics, and category tiles like Science, History, Business, Psychology, and Technology.
    
    Scroll to browse curated content and the slogan: "Real world stories, in a fun way for curious minds."
    
    Click **Explore Comics →** to enter the library.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Library" icon="book-open">
    Access your full collection here.
    
    View personalized recommendations, customize comics with your child as the lead character, and use print-on-demand options.
    
    Categories are prominently displayed for easy browsing.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Search and Browse Comics

Discover comics tailored for curious minds.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use Search" icon="search">
    Click the **Search** icon or magnifying glass.
    
    Type keywords like "science" or "business leaders".
    
    Results show relevant comics from IITians and MBAs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse Categories" icon="layers">
    From the home or library, click tiles:
    
    | Category   | Description                  |
    |------------|------------------------------|
    | Science    | Curated by IITians           |
    | History    | Real-world events            |
    | Business   | Future entrepreneurs         |
    | Psychology | Building EQ for AI world     |
    | Technology | Innovation stories           |
    
    Each leads to themed comics.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Launch Your First Comic

<Callout kind="tip">

Select a comic thumbnail to launch the reader. Customize by adding your child's name as the lead character for a personalized experience.

</Callout>

1. Choose a comic from search or categories.
2. Click **Read** or the play icon.
3. Enjoy the interactive reader—zoom, share pages, or print.
4. Use navigation arrows to progress through panels.
